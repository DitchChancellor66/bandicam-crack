## FAQ

**Is the recorder included?**
No — activator only, 39.9 MB. Bandicam itself comes from the vendor, free build, installed as usual.

**What exactly does the free build limit?**
Two things that matter: the watermark on every recording and a 10-minute stop. Both are local branches in `bdcam.exe`, both are patched here. The 10-minute cap matters most for lectures and gameplay sessions.

**Which versions are supported?**
8.x, and 7.x on the same gate with ⚠️. Point updates of 8.x survive a re-patch. A new major version needs a matching release.

**Do I need an account or a paid key?**
No. The generated serial is accepted offline. No bandicam.com login, no e-mail confirmation.

**Do I have to block the vendor in hosts?**
Not mandatory. If a build flips back to "trial", let the helper write the block lines, reboot, patch again. The exact lines are in the envelope below.

**Windows Defender?**
Expected — a patcher rewrites an executable. Exclusion for the folder, restore from quarantine if it was eaten, patch again.

**Will it keep working after an update?**
Point updates: yes, patched bytes are untouched. Major version: reinstall of the folder, patch again, a minute of work.

**Does it record in 4K now?**
The 4K and HEVC presets stop asking for an upgrade. Real 4K needs a 4K source and a machine that can encode it — that part is on your hardware.

**Will it break my settings or old recordings?**
No. Config, hotkeys and finished files stay as they are.

**Two PCs?**
Yes, one install each, the serial is not seat-limited.

**Why "bdcam.exe not found"?**
Not installed, or a non-default folder. Point the tool at the real folder — the status bar names the reason instead of patching the wrong file.

**Is there a Telegram gate or a "unlock full version" upsell?**
No. MIT, single exe, free.
